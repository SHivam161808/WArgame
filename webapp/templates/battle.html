How to play
  1. Claim a formation slot. The first player of a force is its Commander.
  2. Build each formation within the inventory, set doctrine and shape, then Place it inside your zone.
  3. Place the HVT inside your zone and press READY. The battle starts when both forces are ready.
  4. In battle: click a unit → plot routes, change doctrine, call fire missions, or Take control to drive and shoot it yourself.
  5. Find and destroy the enemy HVT before the time limit while protecting your own.
